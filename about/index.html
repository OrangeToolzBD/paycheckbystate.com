About Paycheck by State

Paycheck by State is a free set of paycheck and income tax calculators that apply the IRS 2026 withholding tables, Social Security and Medicare rules, and each state’s published 2026 income tax brackets.

This site is run by Paycheck by State. It exists because a pay stub shows the result of paycheck math but not how each line was reached. The tools here show the working: which IRS table set your federal withholding, how the Social Security wage base applies, and how your state’s brackets turn salary into take-home pay.

Everything is free and needs no account. The calculators run in your browser, so the figures you type are not sent to us. Each tool names its sources and tax year, and the methodology page spells out every formula, including what the tools leave out, such as local income taxes and state payroll deductions.

The results are estimates for general information, not tax, legal or payroll advice. Your employer’s payroll system and your tax return decide the real numbers.

Corrections

If a figure looks wrong, or a source has published a newer number, please write to the address on the Contact page with the page, the figure and, if you have it, the source. Every report is checked against the original publication. Confirmed errors are fixed on the page, the “Updated” date is changed, and a calculator fix is re-tested before it goes live. The full data set is also reviewed each January when new tax tables are released.

If advertising or affiliate links are ever added, they will be disclosed on the pages where they appear and in the privacy policy.
