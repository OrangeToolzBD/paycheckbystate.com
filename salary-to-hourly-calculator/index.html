Salary to Hourly Calculator

A salary to hourly calculator divides yearly pay by the hours you are paid for. At 40 hours a week for 52 weeks that is 2,080 hours, so $60,000 a year works out to $28.85 an hour, and $25 an hour comes to $52,000 a year before taxes. Change the hours or paid weeks to match your job.

How do you convert a salary to an hourly rate?

To convert a salary to an hourly rate, divide the yearly salary by hours per week times paid weeks per year. For a standard full-time schedule the divisor is 2,080, so $60,000 ÷ 2,080 = $28.85 an hour. To go the other way, multiply the hourly rate by the same 2,080 hours.

How many working hours are in a year?

A full-time year is usually counted as 2,080 hours: 40 hours × 52 weeks. The calendar itself varies a little. 2026 has 261 weekdays, which is 2,088 hours at eight hours a day, and any unpaid weeks reduce the total. That is why the tool lets you set both hours and paid weeks.

The choice of divisor changes the answer:

  • 2,080 hours (40 × 52): $60,000 = $28.85 an hour.
  • 2,088 hours (every 2026 weekday at 8 hours): $60,000 = $28.74 an hour.
  • 2,000 hours (40 × 50, two unpaid weeks): $60,000 = $30.00 an hour.

Paid holidays and paid vacation stay in the count, because the salary covers them. Leave “paid weeks” at 52 if your time off is paid. If you are paid only for weeks you work, such as a contract or seasonal role, enter the weeks you expect to be paid so the hourly figure reflects your real rate.

Is a salary or an hourly wage better?

Neither is better in every case. The biggest practical difference is overtime: federal law requires time and a half for hours over 40 in a workweek for covered employees, while many salaried staff are exempt. An hourly job can out-earn a higher-looking salary if you regularly work long weeks.

Under the Fair Labor Standards Act, covered, non-exempt employees must get at least 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek (U.S. Department of Labor, overtime pay). Being paid a salary does not on its own make you exempt. The Labor Department’s Fact Sheet 17A (revised September 2019) says exempt employees generally must meet job-duties tests and be paid on a salary basis of at least $684 a week, which is $35,568 over 52 weeks.

Points to weigh when comparing offers:

  • Long weeks. A salaried exempt worker earns the same for 50 hours as for 40, so the true hourly rate falls. $60,000 over 50 hours a week is $23.08 an hour.
  • Benefits. Paid time off, health coverage and retirement matches can be worth more than a small difference in base pay.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $25 an hour per year?

$25 an hour is $52,000 a year at 40 hours a week for 52 paid weeks, before taxes. That equals $4,333.33 a month, $2,000 every two weeks or $1,000 a week. If you are paid for fewer weeks, multiply $25 by 40 and then by your paid weeks instead.

What is $60,000 a year per hour?

$60,000 a year is about $28.85 an hour on a 40-hour week with 52 paid weeks, which is 2,080 hours. If two weeks are unpaid the rate rises to $30.00 an hour, and if you regularly work 50 hours a week on the same salary it falls to about $23.08.

How much is $30 an hour per year?

$30 an hour is $62,400 a year at 40 hours a week for 52 paid weeks, before taxes. That is $5,200 a month, $2,400 every two weeks or $1,200 a week. For part-time hours, multiply $30 by your weekly hours and then by 52.
