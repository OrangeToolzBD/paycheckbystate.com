Timesheet Calculator

A timesheet calculator totals clock-out minus clock-in time for each shift, less unpaid breaks. Five days from 8:30 to 17:00 with 30-minute unpaid lunches come to 40 hours, or $880 at $22 an hour. Federal law requires time and a half for hours over 40 in a workweek (U.S. Department of Labor).

Updated September 20, 2026 · By Paycheck by State

How do you calculate hours worked?

For each shift, subtract the start time from the end time, then subtract any unpaid break. Add the shifts for the week. Using 24-hour times avoids a.m. and p.m. mistakes: 08:30 to 17:00 is 8.5 hours, and a 30-minute unpaid lunch leaves 8 hours.

Overnight shifts cross midnight, so add 24 hours when the end time is earlier than the start. A 22:00 to 06:00 shift is 8 hours, and the calculator handles this for you.

Overtime is counted per workweek. The Department of Labor defines a workweek as a fixed, recurring 168 hours and says hours cannot be averaged over two or more weeks. At $22 an hour, a 45-hour week pays 40 × $22 plus 5 × $33, or $1,045. The tool pays 1.5× on every hour past your threshold; for double time, use the overtime calculator.

How do you convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60. Fifteen minutes is 0.25 hours, 30 minutes is 0.5 and 45 minutes is 0.75. So 7 hours 45 minutes is 7.75 hours, not 7.45. Payroll multiplies your rate by the decimal figure, which is why the conversion matters.

Are breaks paid time?

Short breaks usually are; real meal breaks usually are not. The Department of Labor says federal law does not require breaks, but when an employer offers short ones of about 5 to 20 minutes, they count as paid work time. Meal periods of 30 minutes or more generally do not.

A meal period is unpaid only if you are completely relieved from duty, according to DOL Fact Sheet #22 (revised July 2008). Eating at your desk while answering calls is still work time.

Some states set their own rest and meal break rules, so check your state labor department. In this calculator, enter only unpaid break minutes in the break column. Leave paid rest breaks out, or your total will come up short.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours is 9:00 to 3:00?

Six hours, if you mean 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. with no unpaid break. In 24-hour time that is 09:00 to 15:00, and 15 minus 9 is 6. Take a 30-minute unpaid lunch out and you have 5.5 paid hours. At $22 an hour, six hours is $132 before taxes.

How many hours are 7:30 to 3:30?

Eight hours from 7:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. Written as 07:30 to 15:30, the difference is exactly 8. A 30-minute unpaid lunch makes it 7.5 paid hours. Five of those shifts with lunch come to 37.5 hours a week, which stays under the federal 40-hour overtime threshold.

How many hours is 9:30 to 4:30?

Seven hours from 9:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., or 09:30 to 16:30 in 24-hour time. Subtract a 30-minute unpaid lunch and 6.5 hours remain. Enter the times and the break in the calculator to total several days at once.

How do I convert hours of PTO to days?

Divide your PTO hours by the hours in your normal workday. With 8-hour days, 24 hours of PTO is 3 days; with 7.5-hour days, 30 hours is 4 days. The rules for earning and paying PTO come from your employer, because the FLSA does not require paid vacation.
