Where the numbers come from

Every figure on this site traces back to a named, dated source. Federal withholding follows IRS Publication 15-T (2026). The 2026 brackets and standard deductions come from the IRS inflation adjustments released October 9, 2025. Bonus withholding uses the supplemental wage rates in IRS Publication 15. Social Security uses the 2026 wage base of $184,500, and Medicare follows IRS Topic 751. State brackets, deductions and exemptions come from the Tax Foundation table as of January 1, 2026.

The tax tables are refreshed every January, when the IRS and Tax Foundation publish new figures, and sooner if a source issues a correction. The site is maintained by Paycheck by State; see about for the correction policy and the methodology for every formula.
