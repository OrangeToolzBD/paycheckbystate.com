New Hampshire Paycheck Calculator

In New Hampshire, a single worker earning $65,000 a year and paid every two weeks takes home about $2,092.60 per paycheck in 2026: $216.15 federal withholding (IRS Pub 15-T), $191.25 Social Security and Medicare, and no state income tax, because New Hampshire does not tax wages.

Updated September 20, 2026 · By Paycheck by State

New Hampshire income tax brackets for 2026

New Hampshire does not tax wage income, so only federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare come out of a paycheck here, plus any local or payroll taxes listed below.
