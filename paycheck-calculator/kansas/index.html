Kansas Paycheck Calculator

In Kansas, a single worker earning $65,000 a year and paid every two weeks takes home about $1,983.85 per paycheck in 2026: $216.15 federal withholding (IRS Pub 15-T), $191.25 Social Security and Medicare, and about $108.74 of Kansas income tax (Tax Foundation 2026 brackets).
