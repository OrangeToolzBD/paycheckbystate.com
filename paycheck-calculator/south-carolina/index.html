South Carolina Paycheck Calculator

In South Carolina, a single worker earning $65,000 a year and paid every two weeks takes home about $1,987.10 per paycheck in 2026: $216.15 federal withholding (IRS Pub 15-T), $191.25 Social Security and Medicare, and about $105.50 of South Carolina income tax (Tax Foundation 2026 brackets).
