Mississippi Paycheck Calculator

In Mississippi, a single worker earning $65,000 a year and paid every two weeks takes home about $2,020.75 per paycheck in 2026: $216.15 federal withholding (IRS Pub 15-T), $191.25 Social Security and Medicare, and about $71.85 of Mississippi income tax (Tax Foundation 2026 brackets).
