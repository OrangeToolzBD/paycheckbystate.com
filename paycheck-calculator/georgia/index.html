Georgia Paycheck Calculator

Does Georgia have local income taxes?

No. Georgia cities and counties do not withhold an income tax from wages. The Tax Foundation’s 2026 state income tax table does not list Georgia among the states with county- or city-level income taxes, so the state income tax is the only one beyond federal on a Georgia paycheck.

That state tax changed during 2026. The Georgia Department of Revenue’s 2026 Employer’s Withholding Tax Guide (revised June 2026) says HB 463 cut the flat rate from 5.19% to 4.99%, retroactive to January 1, 2026. It also raised the standard deduction to $15,000 for single filers and $30,000 for married couples filing jointly. The Tax Foundation table dated January 1 still shows the old 5.19% rate and $12,000 deduction.

The rate table and take-home figures above already use the new rate and deduction. Under the old rules (5.19% and a $12,000 deduction) the state tax on a $65,000 salary would be about $105.80 per biweekly check; under HB 463 it is about $95.96. At $40,000 it falls from $55.89 to $47.98, and at $100,000 from $175.66 to $163.13. These are estimates of the annual tax spread over 26 checks; your employer’s withholding tables may round differently.

Employers had to keep withholding at 5.19% until the change took effect and could switch to 4.99% starting May 11, 2026, according to the guide.

What else comes out of a paycheck in Georgia?

The Georgia-specific piece of your paycheck is the state income tax, and the form that controls it is Form G-4. The Department of Revenue says the G-4 is completed and given to your employer “in order to have tax withheld from your wages.”

The 2026 employer guide explains what happens around that form. You should file both the G-4 and the federal W-4. If you do not turn in a G-4, your employer can use your federal election when it has enough information; otherwise it withholds as if you were single with zero allowances. You can update your G-4 as often as you like, and a new one takes effect with the first payroll after your employer receives it. Forms claiming more than 14 allowances or exemption from withholding go to the department for approval.

For bonuses, the guide says 2026 payments are withheld at the income tax rate in effect when they are paid, so a bonus paid after the change can be withheld at 4.99%.

Subtract any health premiums, 401(k) deferrals and other elections from the figures above, or model them in the paycheck calculator. The bonus tax calculator covers the federal side of a bonus.
