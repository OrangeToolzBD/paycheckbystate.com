Ohio Paycheck Calculator

In Ohio, a single worker earning $65,000 a year and paid every two weeks takes home about $2,053.94 per paycheck in 2026: $216.15 federal withholding (IRS Pub 15-T), $191.25 Social Security and Medicare, and about $38.66 of Ohio income tax (Tax Foundation 2026 brackets).

Does Ohio have local income taxes?

Yes, often two of them. Many Ohio cities and villages levy a municipal income tax, and many school districts levy a school district income tax. The Tax Foundation’s 2026 table puts Ohio’s average local income tax at 1.2% of AGI (2023 data), which on many paychecks is more than the state tax. This calculator deducts neither.

Municipal income tax

Each city sets its own rate. Columbus, for example, charges 2.5%, and its Income Tax Division says you may owe it “if you live or work in the City of Columbus.” Residents who work elsewhere get a credit, so they never pay Columbus more than 2.5%. On a $2,500 check, 2.5% is $62.50.

School district income tax

This one follows your home address. The Ohio Department of Taxation’s 2026 withholding guidelines require employers to withhold it “for any employee who resides in a taxing school district,” even if the employer does no business there. Some districts tax a traditional base that allows exemptions; others use an earned income base at a flat rate with no exemptions. You can confirm your district and its four-digit code with the department’s tool, The Finder, at tax.ohio.gov/finder.

What else comes out of a paycheck in Ohio?

Beyond federal taxes and the state income tax in the table above, the Ohio-specific items are the local taxes described in the previous section, which this calculator does not deduct. Subtract your city and school district amounts from the take-home figures.

The form that sets them up is the Ohio IT 4. The Department of Taxation’s 2026 guidelines say each employee must complete one, or the employer must withhold as if the employee claimed zero exemptions. The IT 4 also records your school district of residence, and you must update it when that information changes, for example after a move.

Ohio has reciprocity with its five neighbors. The same 2026 guidelines say Ohio withholding is not required for residents of Michigan, Indiana, Kentucky, West Virginia or Pennsylvania earning pay for work in Ohio. Those workers use the combined IT 4 to state their residency, which replaced the old IT 4NR.

After that, your stub shows whatever you elect: health premiums, 401(k) deferrals, HSA contributions and union dues. Pre-tax elections reduce your federal withholding, so try them in the paycheck calculator, and see the methodology for exactly what the estimate includes.
