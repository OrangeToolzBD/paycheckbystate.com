New York Paycheck Calculator

In New York, a single worker earning $65,000 a year and paid every two weeks takes home about $1,980.56 per paycheck in 2026: $216.15 federal withholding (IRS Pub 15-T), $191.25 Social Security and Medicare, and about $112.04 of New York income tax (Tax Foundation 2026 brackets).

Does New York have local income taxes?

Yes, in two cities. New York City and Yonkers both tax wages on top of the state income tax, and this calculator does not include either one. The Tax Foundation’s 2026 table puts New York’s average local income tax at 1.60% of AGI (2023 data).

New York City taxes residents only. The New York tax department states that “nonresidents of New York City are not liable for New York City personal income tax,” so commuting into Manhattan does not trigger it. For 2026, employers use publication NYS-50-T-NYC (1/26), whose supplemental rate for bonuses paid separately is 4.25%.

Yonkers taxes both groups. Residents pay a surcharge that the Yonkers withholding publication NYS-50-T-Y (1/26) computes as 16.75% of the state withholding figure. People who work in Yonkers but live elsewhere may owe the nonresident earnings tax; the supplemental rate for nonresidents is 0.50% for 2026, and the department notes that rate “has not changed.”

Your employer learns which of these apply from Form IT-2104. The 2026 IT-2104 instructions say it tells your employer how much state, city and Yonkers tax to withhold, and to file a new one if you move into or out of either city. The state also revised its 2026 withholding tables for rate cuts (tax.ny.gov).

What else comes out of a paycheck in New York?

Two small state insurance deductions appear on most New York pay stubs, and this calculator deducts neither, so subtract them from the figures above.

Paid Family Leave

For 2026, the state’s Paid Family Leave program sets the employee contribution at 0.432% of gross wages per pay period, up to a maximum of $411.91 for the year. On a $65,000 salary that is $280.80 a year, or $10.80 per biweekly check. The same page puts the 2026 maximum weekly benefit at $1,228.53.

Disability benefits

The Workers’ Compensation Board says an employee’s disability benefits contribution is “one-half of one percent” of wages, but “no more than sixty cents a week.” Employers may collect it but are not required to. At the cap, that is $1.20 per biweekly check.

After those come the federal items already in the table, plus anything you elect: health premiums, retirement deferrals, commuter benefits and union dues. If you live in New York City or Yonkers, subtract the local tax described above as well. For a bonus, the bonus tax calculator shows the federal flat-rate math.
